# Code of Conduct

Project participation must remain respectful, constructive, and safe. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Contributors should focus review on behavior and evidence, assume good faith while remaining rigorous, and make space for different levels of experience. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information.
